Snap a grocery receipt, get your kitchen list

Typing every grocery into an app gets old fast, which is why so many kitchen lists get abandoned. Tum Tum skips the typing: take one photo of your grocery receipt and the foods go on your kitchen list, each in a likely place you can change.

How the receipt scanner works

  1. Snap the receipt. One photo, or two for a long receipt. You can also open a receipt you already have as a picture or PDF, for example one a store emailed you.
  2. Tum Tum reads it. In a few moments you see the store, each item with a plain name, the price you paid and the amount where the receipt shows one.
  3. Check and confirm. Everything is on one screen. Tap any item to fix a name, a place or an amount. Press Confirm and the food is in your Kitchen.

It knows food from household stuff

Paper towels, dish soap and batteries are on your receipt too, but they don't belong in your fridge list. Tum Tum keeps them in a separate group called "Not going to Kitchen", so your list stays food only. If it gets one wrong, one tap moves it in.

Short receipt names, real food names

Receipts love codes like "KS ORG BNNA 3LB". Tum Tum turns them into words you would use yourself, like "Bananas", and suggests a place for each one: fridge, freezer, pantry or counter.

No receipt? Snap the groceries

Farmers market, a friend's garden, or you threw the receipt away? Put the food on the table and take a photo. Tum Tum names the foods it sees and adds them to your list. A groceries photo has no prices or amounts, so you can add those yourself if you want.

What happens to the photo

Our server passes the photo to an outside service to be read and does not keep the photo. Your Kitchen list is saved on your iPhone. How long the outside service keeps a photo, and what our server keeps, is in our privacy policy.

Good to know

  • Scanning receipts and grocery photos comes with Tum Tum Plus, which has a free trial for new subscribers. Adding food by hand, by voice or by barcode is free.
  • Receipts read better flat on a table in good light. If a line is hard to read, Tum Tum says some lines may be missing so you can add them.
  • No account and no sign-up. Open the app and scan.
