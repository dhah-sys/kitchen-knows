Dinner ideas from what you already have

"What's for dinner?" is easier to answer when you know what you have. Because Tum Tum already knows your kitchen, it can suggest meals you can actually make tonight, without another trip to the store.

Ideas built from your kitchen

Open Meals and you see dinner ideas that match the food on your list. Tum Tum shows how many of the ingredients you already have, and favors recipes that use the foods that need eating first.

New ideas every day

The ideas follow what is in your kitchen, and a "Try something new" row brings different dinners every day, so dinner does not turn into the same three meals on repeat. If a recipe needs a few things you don't have, add what's missing to your shopping list in a couple of taps.

Cook step by step

Start cooking and Tum Tum shows one step at a time, in big text you can read from across the counter. It can read the steps out loud and set timers, so your hands stay free.

Keep the recipes you love

Found a recipe you love online? Paste the link and Tum Tum brings it in. Meals you cook are kept so you can make them again, with a photo of how yours turned out.

After dinner

When you finish cooking, Tum Tum asks which foods you used up, which have some left and which you skipped, and updates your kitchen list, so tomorrow's ideas are right too.

Meet Tum Tum

Snap your groceries. Know what you have. Eat it before it goes bad. No account, no ads.

Coming soon on iPhone

Scanning receipts and grocery photos comes with Tum Tum Plus, with a free trial. Your kitchen list, shopping list and dinner ideas are free.
