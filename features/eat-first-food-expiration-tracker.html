Know what to eat first, every day

Most food goes bad because it was forgotten, not because nobody wanted it. The spinach hides behind the milk, the yogurt slips to the back. Tum Tum puts the foods that need eating first right in front of you.

The Use soon list

Every day, Tum Tum looks at what is in your kitchen and how long each food usually keeps in its place. The few foods that need eating first show at the top of your Kitchen and on the Today screen. It is a short list on purpose, so it feels doable.

Still good? One tap

When a food reaches its usual limit, Tum Tum asks you to check it instead of guessing. Looked fine? Tap "Still good". Ate it? Swipe it as eaten. Gone off? Swipe it as tossed. That way the list matches your real fridge.

Reminders that don't nag

If you turn them on, Tum Tum sends at most three reminders a week, usually one or two, mostly a quick check on a food that needs eating. Ignore a few and it backs off. No daily alarms, no guilt.

Right on your home screen

The Tum Tum widget shows what to eat soon without opening the app. Tap it to open your Use soon list.

From "eat first" to dinner

Knowing the zucchini needs using is a start. Dinner is easier with a plan. Tum Tum's dinner ideas start with the foods that need eating first.
