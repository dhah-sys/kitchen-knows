A shopping list that knows what's at home

A classic grocery mistake is buying something you already have at home. Tum Tum's shopping list is connected to your kitchen list, so it can tell you before you double up.

It knows what you have

Add "rice" and Tum Tum shows you if there is already some in your kitchen. You decide whether to keep it on the list. No more three open jars of the same thing.

Add things the easy way

  • Type what you need.
  • Add the missing ingredients from a recipe in a couple of taps.
  • When you finish a food, Tum Tum offers to put it on the list for next time.

In the store

Tick items off and they move to "In your cart". When you get home, snap the receipt and what you bought comes off your shopping list and goes into your kitchen. Nothing to type twice.

Free, with no account

The shopping list is free to use. No sign-up, no ads.

Meet Tum Tum

Snap your groceries. Know what you have. Eat it before it goes bad. No account, no ads.
