Your fridge and pantry, in your pocket

Standing in the store wondering if you still have eggs is a small daily annoyance. Tum Tum keeps a list of the food in your home, sorted by where it lives, so the answer is always one look away.

Four places, like your real kitchen

Every food sits in one of four places: Fridge, Freezer, Pantry or Counter. Tap a tab to see just that place, or All to see everything. Moved the chicken to the freezer? Change its place with a tap.

Filling the list takes seconds

  • Snap a grocery receipt or a photo of the groceries (with Tum Tum Plus).
  • Type a food, or just say it: "a dozen eggs in the fridge".
  • Scan a barcode on a packet.

Keeping it true without effort

A list only helps if it stays right. When you finish something, swipe it as eaten. If something went bad, swipe it as tossed. You can also tell Tum Tum in plain words, like "I used the milk", and it updates the list for you.

See how fresh things are

Each food shows a simple freshness note, such as "Best by Friday" or "Check if still good", based on USDA food storage guidance for that food and place. Foods that need eating soon float to the top in a short Use soon list.

Search, and a widget

Search your kitchen in a second. Add the home screen widget and the foods to eat first are right there next to your other apps.

Private by design

Your kitchen list is saved on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. You can delete everything at any time in Settings.

Meet Tum Tum

Snap your groceries. Know what you have. Eat it before it goes bad. No account, no ads.
