Snap your grocery receipt. Tum Tum sorts your kitchen and tells you what to eat before it goes bad.

For busy households who throw out forgotten food, Tum Tum is the pantry app that builds your kitchen list from one receipt photo. Unlike apps where you type everything, it does it in one snap and tells you what to eat before it goes bad.

Why people keep it

No typing

Take one photo of your receipt. Every food lands on your kitchen list, each in the fridge, freezer, pantry or counter. Change any place with a tap.

The foods that need eating first sit at the top under Use soon, so nothing hides at the back of the fridge.

🗑️

Less food in the bin

When you can see what needs eating, you eat it instead of tossing it. Less food wasted means less money thrown out with it.

How it works

  1. Snap the receipt

    One photo, or two for a long receipt. A receipt a store emailed you works too.

  2. Check the list

    Everything is on one screen. Tap any item to fix it. Paper towels and soap stay off your food list.

  3. Put it away

    Press Confirm. Your food goes into your Kitchen, sorted into fridge, freezer, pantry and counter.

Then Tum Tum shows "Use soon"

Each day the foods to eat first are right on the Today screen. One tap says you ate it, it's still good, or it was tossed. Turn on reminders and Tum Tum gives you a nudge before a food goes bad.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Scanning receipts comes with Tum Tum Plus: $29.99 a year or $5.99 a month (US prices), paid through Apple. The yearly plan starts with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers, with 5 scans to try. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Your kitchen list, shopping list and dinner ideas are free, and so is adding food by hand.

What happens to my receipt photo?

The photo leaves your iPhone only when you scan it. Our server passes it to an outside service to be read and does not keep the photo. What was read (the store, items, prices and date) stays on our server for 30 days, so scanning the same photo again is free. Your kitchen list is saved on your iPhone. The privacy policy has every detail.

Is it only for iPhone?

Yes. Tum Tum is an iPhone app and needs iOS 26.5 or later. There is no Android version.

Does it work with any store's receipt?

Yes. Snap a printed receipt from any grocery store, or open a receipt a store emailed you as a PDF or picture. Receipts read best flat on a table in good light. If a line is hard to read, Tum Tum tells you some lines may be missing so you can add them.

What if it gets an item wrong?

Before you press Confirm, tap any item to fix its name, place or amount, or remove it. Household things like paper towels stay out of your food list, and one tap moves one in if Tum Tum got it wrong.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-up and no ads. Open the app and scan.
