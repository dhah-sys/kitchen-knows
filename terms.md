## Estimates, not promises

Best-by dates, leftover "eat by" days, nutrition numbers, daily calorie and nutrient goals in My plate, what eaten food cost and recipe ideas are estimates to help you plan. They are not food-safety, medical or diet advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to how you eat. When in doubt about a food, check it yourself or throw it out.

## AI reading can make mistakes

Receipts and grocery photos are read by an AI. You check the list before anything goes into your Kitchen, and you can change or remove any item. Tum Tum isn't responsible for decisions made from a misread photo.

## Recipes and cooking

Recipes come from TheMealDB, a public recipe collection, or from links you import yourself. Check every recipe's ingredients for allergies and dietary needs before cooking. Cooking timers and step-by-step mode are helpers: keep an eye on your cooking, especially with heat.

## Tum Tum Plus

- Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, yearly or monthly, at the price shown before you buy. Payment is taken by Apple when you confirm the purchase.
- It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in **iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions**.
- Reading receipts and grocery photos, and AI cooking answers, come with Plus. Without Plus, Tum Tum reads no receipts or grocery photos and answers cooking questions on your iPhone only. A receipt or a photo of groceries counts as one scan.
- The yearly plan may start with a free trial, shown before you buy. During the trial Plus reads up to 5 scans; the full limits start when the trial ends and the plan is paid.
- Plus reads up to 30 scans in 30 days and 5 in an hour, shared by up to 5 iPhones on the same Apple ID, so that one person's use stays fair for everyone. A read with more than 50 items counts as 2.
- Your Kitchen, garden, recipes and shopping list stay free with or without Plus.

## Fair use

Please send only your own grocery receipts and grocery photos, and don't try to get around Tum Tum's limits. We may limit or stop service for misuse.
