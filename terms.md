## Estimates, not promises

Best-by dates marked "USDA estimate", leftover "eat by" days, nutrition numbers, what eaten food cost and recipe ideas are estimates to help you plan. They are not food-safety or medical advice. When in doubt about a food, check it yourself or throw it out.

## AI reading can make mistakes

Receipts and grocery photos are read by an AI. You check the list before anything goes into your Kitchen, and you can change or remove any item. Kitchen Knows isn't responsible for decisions made from a misread photo.

## Recipes and cooking

Recipes come from TheMealDB, a public recipe collection. Check every recipe's ingredients for allergies and dietary needs before cooking. Cooking timers and step-by-step mode are helpers: keep an eye on your cooking, especially with heat.

## Kitchen Knows Plus

- Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, yearly or monthly, at the price shown before you buy. Payment is taken by Apple when you confirm the purchase.
- It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in **iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions**.
- Plus includes up to the number of receipts a month shown on the plan screen, so that one person's use stays fair for everyone.
