# Support

Kitchen Knows turns your grocery receipts into a kitchen list and helps you eat food while it's still good.

## Get help

- In the app: **Settings > Help & privacy > Send feedback**
- Email: **dhiraj.bayar@gmail.com**

We usually reply within two working days.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app, open **Settings > Help & privacy > Delete all my data**. Everything on your iPhone is removed. Server records about receipts expire on their own; to have them removed sooner, send us a message.

**How do I cancel Kitchen Knows Plus?**
Open **iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Kitchen Knows**. Deleting the app or your data does not cancel a subscription.

**I bought Plus but the app doesn't show it.**
In the app, open the Plus screen and tap **Restore purchases**.

**Where do my receipt photos go?**
Only when you scan, and only after you agree, the photo goes through our server to Anthropic's Claude AI to be read. We don't keep the photo. Details are in the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

**A receipt was read wrong.**
You can fix or remove any item before tapping Confirm, and edit any food later in Kitchen.

**My cooking timer didn't ring.**
Timers ring as alarms only if you allowed alarms the first time you started one. You can turn that permission on in **iPhone Settings > Kitchen Knows**. Without it, you get a notification with a sound instead, as long as notifications are on.
