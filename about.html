About Tum Tum

Tum Tum is a small, independent iPhone app made by Dhiraj Pandey. It turns a photo of your groceries into a kitchen list and helps you eat food while it is still good.

Why it exists

Food often goes to waste at home because it is forgotten, not because nobody wanted it. A kitchen list fixes that, but nobody keeps one by hand for long. Tum Tum makes the list from a receipt or a photo, keeps it sorted by fridge, freezer, pantry and counter, and puts the foods that need eating first in front of you.

How it is made

  • No account, no ads and no ad tracking. Your kitchen list is saved on your iPhone. The privacy policy explains exactly what leaves the phone and why.
  • Freshness notes in the app are based on USDA food storage guidance.
  • Tum Tum himself, the little tomato, is the app's guide. He also grows a garden as you eat your food.

How our kitchen guides are written

The blog covers practical questions like how long leftovers last and what date labels mean. Every food-safety fact comes from a public source such as the FDA, USDA or a university extension service, and each guide lists its sources at the end. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we will fix it.
